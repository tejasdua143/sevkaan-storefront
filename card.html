About Steam Wallet

Steam is the largest digital distribution platform for PC games, with a library of over 50,000 titles from indie gems to AAA blockbusters. A Steam Wallet gift card adds funds directly to your Steam account — usable for games, DLC, in-game items (CS2 skins, Dota 2 cosmetics, TF2 hats), software, and even hardware. Funds never expire.
